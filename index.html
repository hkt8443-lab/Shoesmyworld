Our Story

Shoes That
Last a Lifetime

STRÏDE was born in a small atelier in Porto, Portugal, where master cobblers have shaped leather for generations. We believe a shoe isn't fast fashion — it is a commitment. One that should age beautifully with you.

Every pair is hand-lasted on traditional wooden forms, stitched with waxed linen thread, and resoled free of charge after three years. Because craftsmanship shouldn't expire.

  • Full-grain leather uppers from Limerick tanneries
  • Cork & wool insoles for natural breathability
  • Goodyear-welted construction for easy resoling
  • Plant-based pigment dyes — no harsh chemicals
  • Each pair takes 14 hours to build by hand

The Making

Every Pair Is
a Week's Work

I

Pattern & Cutting

We cut each upper by hand from a single hide, reading the grain for strength and beauty before committing scissors to leather.

II

Lasting & Welting

Uppers are pulled over a wooden last and Goodyear-welted — a double row of stitching that outlasts the leather itself.

III

Sole Construction

Cork fillers and rubber soles are hand-pressed and edge-finished on a spinning wheel, then burnished to a mirror edge.

IV

Finishing & Quality

Each pair is hand-finished with natural wax, inspected against 48-point quality criteria, and rested for 24 hours before boxing.
